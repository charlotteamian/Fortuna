### 描述

```text
Fortuna 是一款本地优先的个人资产管理工具，帮助你统一记录银行存款、现金、基金、股票与 ETF、贵金属、债券、房产、数字货币、信用卡和贷款等资产负债信息。

主要功能：
• 跨账户记录资产、负债与净资产

• 导出和恢复完整 Excel 备份
• 使用系统分享保存图片报告和备份文件
• 可选的信用卡还款日历提醒
• 默认隐藏金额，便于在公共场合查看

隐私与边界：
Fortuna 不要求登录，不包含广告、分析 SDK 或用户跟踪。资产账本默认保存在设备本地。行情查询会向独立数据服务发送查询所需的公开币种、金属、基金或证券代码，但不会有意发送账户名称、余额、数量、交易记录或备注。导出、分享和日历提醒都需要你主动操作或通过系统界面确认。

Fortuna 是记录与估算工具，不提供贷款、授信、证券或数字货币交易，也不提供个性化投资、税务、法律或借贷建议。行情和计算可能延迟、不完整或不准确，请在重要决策前独立核实。
```

### Description

```text
Fortuna is a local-first personal wealth tracker for recording assets, liabilities, holdings, and net worth across accounts.

Key features:
• Track assets, liabilities, and net worth across multiple accounts
• Manage portfolio cash, holdings, trades, and realised or unrealised P&L
• Analyse allocation by category, institution, and currency
• Review net-worth trends and progress toward allocation plans
• Refresh exchange rates, precious-metal prices, and selected public market quotes
• Export and restore complete Excel backups
• Save image reports and backup files through the system share sheet
• Optionally create credit-card repayment reminders in Calendar
• Hide amounts by default when viewing in public

Privacy and scope:
Fortuna requires no login and includes no advertising, analytics SDK, or user tracking. Your ledger stays on the device by default. Quote lookups send public currency, metal, fund, or security identifiers required for the request to independent data providers, but do not intentionally send account names, balances, quantities, transactions, or notes. Exports, sharing, and calendar reminders occur only after you act or confirm through a system interface.

Fortuna is a record-keeping and estimation tool. It does not provide loans, credit, securities or crypto trading, or personalised investment, tax, legal, or lending advice. Quotes and calculations may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate; verify important figures independently.
```
