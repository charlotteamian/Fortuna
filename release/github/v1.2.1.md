## Highlights / 重点

Fortuna 1.2.1 separates whether an account participates in wealth calculations from whether it is shown in everyday views. You can now keep tracking an asset or liability without counting it in totals, or hide it for a cleaner overview without changing net worth.

Fortuna 1.2.1 将“是否参与资产统计”和“是否在日常页面显示”拆成两个独立控制。现在既可以继续记录某项资产或负债、但不把它计入总额；也可以只隐藏它，让首页更简洁，同时保持净资产计算不变。

## Account controls / 账户控制

- Choose **Include in totals / 计入统计** when creating or editing an asset or liability.
- An excluded item remains visible and editable, but does not contribute to total assets, total liabilities, net worth, or the allocation-plan base.
- Use the new hide action on the overview to remove an item from home, product, and breakdown-chart views.
- Restore hidden assets or liabilities at any time under **Settings → Hidden Assets & Liabilities / 设置 → 已隐藏的资产/负债**.
- Hiding is presentation-only: a hidden item still participates in aggregate totals and total/net-worth trends unless it is separately excluded from totals.

## Consistency and portability / 一致性与数据可携带性

- Overview subtotals, product summaries, allocation plans, charts, and exported reports follow the same inclusion rule.
- Excel backup/restore preserves both settings.
- Portable JSON snapshot schema v7 exposes `includedInTotals` and `hiddenInApp` while keeping the full active ledger available to user-authorised workflows.
- Existing accounts default to included and visible, so upgrading does not silently change current totals.

## Product and platform updates / 产品与平台更新

- Added iOS version guards, current-web-asset verification, privacy manifest, localized calendar permission copy, and App Store preparation materials.

## Privacy / 隐私

Fortuna remains local-first. No Fortuna login, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK is required. Hiding an account is a display preference, not encryption or deletion; financial records remain in the local ledger and complete backups until the user explicitly deletes them.

Fortuna 继续坚持本地优先：不要求 Fortuna 账户登录，也不依赖分析或广告 SDK。隐藏只是显示设置，不等于加密或删除；在用户主动删除前，账本和完整备份仍会保留相关记录。
